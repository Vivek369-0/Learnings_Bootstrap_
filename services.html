Engineered wood often gets compared with traditional solid wood, and many customers wonder if it is a compromise. Wow Craft answer: We select high-quality engineered wood that balances durability, strength, and aesthetics. Combined with metal reinforcements, our products are designed to withstand Indian household usage. A Bangalore-based buyer told us, “I was skeptical at first, but after two years of daily use, the engineered wood table still looks brand new.”
